# 🎥 RewindBoard — Chess TikTok Content & Hook Strategy

This document outlines high-retention TikTok video concepts, scroll-stopping hooks, visual storyboards, and market positioning angles for growing **RewindBoard** through a Chess TikTok channel.

---

## 🎯 Core Positioning & Viral Angles

Chess TikTok thrives on 4 psychological triggers:
1. **Dopamine (`!!` Brilliant Badge Reveals)**: The teal `!!` badge and jumping evaluation bar keep viewers watching until the reveal.
2. **Relatable Pain (Blundering a Winning Position)**: Heart-monitor evaluation graphs (`+6.0` $\rightarrow$ `-M2`) drive massive shares and comments.
3. **Ego & Status (Estimated Performance Elo)**: Players love seeing an engine rate their game `2200 Elo` (or roasting a friend's `400 Elo` game).
4. **"Free Hack" Framing**: Solving the frustration of Chess.com's 1-review-per-day limit without a credit card or login.

## 🎬 Video 1: The "1 Daily Review Limit" Pain Point (Broad Viral Reach)

- **Goal**: Maximum saves, bookmarks, and profile link clicks.
- **Target Audience**: 600–1500 Elo Chess.com players frustrated by the Diamond paywall.
- **Duration**: 18–22 seconds.

### Hook (0–3s)
- **Visual**: Screen recording or over-the-shoulder phone shot hitting the *"Upgrade to Diamond to review this game"* popup on Chess.com, then swiping it away.
- **On-Screen Text**: `POV: You already used your 1 free Game Review today 💀`
- **Spoken Hook**:
  > *"Stop paying \$120 a year just to see if your sacrifice was Brilliant (`!!`)."*

### Body (3–17s)
- **03–08s**: Cut to typing a Chess.com username into **RewindBoard** (`GameImporter`). Show the last 20 games loading in 1 second with Win/Loss badges—no login required.
- **08–13s**: Click **Analyze**. Show the Stockfish 19 WebAssembly progress bar hitting 100%.
- **13–17s**: Cut directly to a move reveal where the teal **`!!` Brilliant** badge pops onto the square, the Coach Banner explains the sacrifice, and the Summary panel shows **Estimated Elo: 2150**.

### Call to Action (17–22s)
- **Spoken CTA**:
  > *"It runs Stockfish 19 right in your browser for free. Link is in my bio—drop your Chess.com username in the comments and I'll review your craziest game next."*

## 🎬 Video 2: "Brilliant (`!!`) or Blunder (`??`)?" Interactive Quiz (Comment Driver)

- **Goal**: Boost watch completion rate (>100% via pauses) and comment debates.
- **Target Audience**: Tactics & puzzle lovers.
- **Duration**: 15–18 seconds.

### Hook (0–3s)
- **Visual**: Zoomed-in view of `ChessReviewBoard` right before a wild Queen or Knight sacrifice. Hover your finger/cursor over the **Next Move** button without clicking it yet.
- **On-Screen Text**: `Pause right now: Is Qxf7+ a Blunder (??) or Brilliant (!!)? 🤔`
- **Spoken Hook**:
  > *"My opponent spammed 'Thanks' in the chat when I left my Queen hanging on move 17… Pause right now: is this a Blunder (`??`) or a Brilliant (`!!`)?"*

### Body (3–14s)
- **03–05s**: Hold the board for 2 seconds so viewers pause and type their guess in the comments.
- **05–10s**: Click **Next Move** $\rightarrow$ reveal the teal **`!!` Brilliant** badge on the square + the vertical `EvalBar` shooting from `+0.4` to `M3`.
- **10–14s**: Play out the 3-move forced checkmate sequence on the board with green best-move SVG arrows visible.

### Call to Action (14–18s)
- **Spoken CTA**:
  > *"Did you see the smothered mate? Test your own sacrifices for free on RewindBoard—link in bio."*

## 🛠️ Filming & Editing Checklist for Maximum Retention

1. **Always Zoom In on the Board + Eval Bar**: On mobile vertical video (`9:16`), crop tightly around `<EvalBar />`, `<ChessReviewBoard />`, and `<CoachBanner />` so viewers can clearly read the `!!` and `??` badges without squinting.
2. **Use Sound Effects on Move Badges**:
   - Add a crisp *ding / sparkle* SFX when a **`!!` Brilliant** badge appears.
   - Add a *vine boom / error* SFX when a **`??` Blunder** drops the evaluation bar.
3. **Pin a Comment on Every Video**:
   - Immediately after posting, pin a comment: *"Try it free on your own games (no login needed): [your-domain] — reply to this comment with your username to get featured!"*
4. **Batch-Record Follower Roasts**:
   - Pick 5 follower usernames from the comments every week, run them through RewindBoard in 2 minutes, and record 5 episodes back-to-back.
